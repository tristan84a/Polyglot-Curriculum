PURPOSE
This syllabus is the canonical introduction map for Standard Latvian. Original exploratory micro-deck cards 5700000–5700014 are preserved unchanged. The seed extends the curriculum to 100 cards. A syllabus unit introduces a branch; it neither closes that branch nor mandates a fixed card quota. Vocabulary, inflection, and natural syntax should develop in a widening spiral.

LANGUAGE-SPECIFIC AUTHORING GUIDANCE
Use modern Latvian orthography, including long vowels ā ē ī ū, consonants č ģ ķ ļ ņ š ž, and normal punctuation. Do not strip macrons or cedillas. InputHint is normally empty.
Latvian has two grammatical genders, masculine and feminine, and normally seven nominal cases: nominative, genitive, dative, accusative, instrumental, locative and vocative. Traditional instrumental forms overlap with accusative in singular and dative in plural, and instrumental function is often expressed with ar. Some noun declensions have stem alternations; introduce productive contrasts with real nouns before unusual paradigms.
Adjectives agree in gender, number and case, and distinguish indefinite versus definite endings. Start with frequent indefinite adjectives; use definite forms when the context motivates them.
There are no English-style definite and indefinite articles. Preserve natural bare noun phrases. Possession is ordinarily expressed by a dative possessor plus ir; negative possession by nav often involves a genitive possessed noun: Man ir grāmata; Man nav grāmatas. Avoid transferring Lithuanian's genitive-under-verbal-negation rule indiscriminately: Latvian commonly retains the accusative object with a negated transitive verb, e.g. Es neredzu māju.
Latvian personal subjects are often expressed, though pro-drop is possible in context. The unmarked written and spoken standard is the baseline. Tu is singular familiar, jūs plural or polite; do not force informal labels into natural English prompts unless essential for disambiguation.
Teach present tense agreement using representative verbs with appropriate stem and ending patterns; progress through past and future without imposing English progressive aspect. Later introduce reflexive -ties verbs, prefixal aspectual/lexical contrasts, participles, and debitive jā- constructions.
Standalone prompts must select an intended exact answer without relying on adjacent rows. Choose idiomatic English and natural contemporary Latvian; revise prompts if distinct intended meanings would collide. Keep citations of verbs in dictionary infinitive form.

MICRO TO SEED
5700000–5700014: preserved original exploratory material. Its accusative forms, possession with a dative possessor and numeral agreement are useful previews, not evidence that all corresponding branches are established.

GENERATION POLICY
Original micro cards are immutable for this graduation. New IDs rise consecutively within 5700000–5799999; CSV row order is canonical introduction order. Avoid duplicate EnglishPrompt entries and exact duplicate cards. New content should interleave useful vocabulary with natural morphology and syntax. A first conjugation or declension paradigm may be dense when each cell teaches a genuine contrast; do not mechanically exhaust the same paradigm for every new stem. Later preemptive examples are permissible but must not falsely advance the recorded frontier. Every card must make sense in isolation after shuffling.

FRONTIER
LV-16 — Core present-tense verb patterns: lasīt, dzert, ēst. The seed previews these patterns; extend their person-number contrasts and broaden everyday verbs before proceeding to full genitive and dative noun paradigms.
