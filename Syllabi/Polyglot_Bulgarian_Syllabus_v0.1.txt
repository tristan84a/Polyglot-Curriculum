PURPOSE
Graduate the 15-card micro opening to a systematic seed deck. Units are canonical introductions, not closed lessons: new grammar and vocabulary continue to reinforce earlier material in a widening spiral. Independent English prompts remain useful once cards are shuffled.

REFERENCE AND AUTHORING CONVENTIONS
Use contemporary Standard Bulgarian in Cyrillic. Targets retain ordinary Bulgarian punctuation and orthography. InputHint is blank. The Bulgarian verb citation form is conventionally the first-person singular present (чета, пиша, работя), not an infinitive: modern Bulgarian has no productive infinitive. Ordinary neutral clauses frequently omit recoverable subject pronouns; overt pronouns in the opening establish person paradigms, not mandatory usage. Bulgarian distinguishes masculine, feminine, neuter and plural noun-phrase agreement. The postposed definite article varies by gender and number and, for masculine singular, syntactic role in formal writing (учителят/учителя); use natural meaningful examples and explicitly mark the distinction where English alone does not determine the form. The literary full/short masculine article rule is an orthographic standard, not an audible case contrast. Bulgarian nouns do not retain the rich productive case inflections of other Slavic languages, though pronouns and vocatives retain distinctions. The particle ли frequently marks polar questions; не precedes ordinary finite verbs. The negative forms of имам are нямам/нямаш and are best learned as a common productive paradigm. Bulgarian has no normal English-style articles preceding the noun; its definite article is suffixed. Unqualified prompts use neutral contemporary standard forms, not regional dialects or Macedonian forms. Polite Вие and capitalisation belong to a later register strand. The reported/past, evidential, aspect, clitics, and verb conjugation systems require gradual, ongoing treatment. EnglishPrompt must be unique and stand alone; IDs stay in 4700000–4799999.
