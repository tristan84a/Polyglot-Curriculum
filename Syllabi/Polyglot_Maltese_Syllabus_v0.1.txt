PURPOSE AND REFERENCE VARIETY
Contemporary standard Maltese (Malta) is the default. Use standard Maltese Latin orthography including ċ, ġ, ħ, għ and ż, and normal punctuation. Distinguish standard written Maltese from informal spoken forms when exact answers differ. The language is Semitic with substantial Romance and English vocabulary: teach productive Maltese patterns, not a simplified Arabic or Italian grammar.

CARD POLICY
The original 15-card micro deck remains unchanged. English prompts must identify distinctions relevant to exact-answer checking. Default InputHint is blank, with short notes only for disambiguation. Keep ordinary natural phrases; avoid treating every English 'is' as requiring an overt copula. Teach article assimilation, gender, noun plurals, verbal person prefixes and suffixes, and discontinuous negation through contrasting sentences. Cards can preview later units without moving the frontier. Retain allocated IDs 5300000–5399999.

SEED COVERAGE AND FRONTIER
The appended seed extends through introductory MT-30 material, but many units are only previewed; do not treat the seed as exhaustive completion. Formal forward frontier: MT-11 — Għand- possession and person suffixes. MT-01–MT-10 are introduced, with longitudinal reinforcement needed. Seed includes early possession, locations, quantities, and present verbs to support useful phrases.

LONGITUDINAL STRANDS
- Definite article assimilation and vowel-initial forms; distinguish article spelling from noun-initial consonants.
- Masculine/feminine agreement; broken plurals and suffix plurals; count forms.
- Semitic root-and-pattern morphology alongside borrowed and irregular verbs.
- Subject marking in imperfect versus suffixes in perfect; irregular high-frequency verbs.
- Ma-...-x negation, contractions and orthographic conventions.
- Possession with għand-, ta' and pronominal suffixes.
- Preposition fusion and pronominal suffixes; natural word order.
- Everyday vocabulary and sociolinguistic registers, including English code-switching only when explicitly labelled.
