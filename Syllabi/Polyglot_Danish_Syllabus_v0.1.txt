PURPOSE

This syllabus defines the canonical pedagogical path for contemporary Danish in Polyglot. It governs systematic development after the standardized 15-card micro opening. Vocabulary grows continuously while grammar develops in a widening spiral rather than in sealed textbook blocks.

REFERENCE VARIETY AND WRITING

Contemporary standard Danish as used in Denmark is the unmarked reference variety. Targets use ordinary modern Danish orthography including æ, ø and å. InputHint is normally blank.

The curriculum initially uses broadly neutral written and spoken-standard Danish. Pronunciation receives explicit longitudinal treatment because the relationship between Danish spelling and ordinary speech cannot be inferred reliably from orthography alone. Regional varieties belong to later comparison work.

COURSE-WIDE CARD PRINCIPLES

1. Write natural ordinary English prompts and natural Danish Targets. Do not make either language imitate the other for flashcard convenience.
2. Apply established course defaults silently. Add a qualifier only when the intended Target deliberately departs from a default and the distinction cannot reasonably be inferred from ordinary English or context.
3. Parenthetical qualifiers are sparse early scaffolding. Prefer natural contextual cues.
4. Exact matching identifies the canonical natural Target selected for a card; it does not claim that no other Danish wording could express the proposition.
5. Noun gender is lexical. Learn common-gender en and neuter et through noun phrases rather than English-side gender labels.
6. Definiteness is expressed both by suffixed articles and by the preposed definite article in modified noun phrases. Treat this as a longitudinal noun-phrase system.
7. Adjective forms interact with gender, definiteness and number. Establish contrasts through useful phrases before broad paradigm synthesis.
8. Danish finite verbs do not agree with person or number. Establish this economy early rather than repeating artificial person paradigms.
9. Main-clause V2 word order is central. Teach it first in neutral subject-initial clauses and then through fronted adverbials and other first-position constituents.
10. Negation with ikke interacts with clause type and word order. Its placement should develop together with V2 and later subordinate-clause structure.
11. Stød and reduced spoken forms matter for listening and pronunciation but should not be represented by artificial target spelling. Teach pronunciation through explicit pronunciation-oriented cards or explanatory contrasts where useful.
12. Establish representative productive patterns clearly; thereafter reduce paradigm density and continue lexical expansion.
13. Avoid commas in card sentences whenever a natural alternative is available because the simple CSV workflow is comma-sensitive.
14. Check the entire live deck plus every new batch for duplicate IDs and duplicate EnglishPrompt values before committing.

MICRO DECK AND SEED TRANSITION

Cards 2600000-2600014 are the standardized 15-card micro opening. They preview suffixed definiteness, en gender, neuter adjective agreement, present verbal morphology and plural nouns before those systems are formally established. Preserve them as footholds rather than treating those syllabus units as complete.

Cards 2600015 onward form the syllabus-governed seed and continuing canonical deck.

STAGE 1 — CORE NOUN PHRASES AND SIMPLE CLAUSES

DA-01 — Core concrete vocabulary
OBJECTIVE: Build varied high-value nouns, adjectives and basic lexical items in ordinary Danish spelling.

DA-02 — Personal pronouns
OBJECTIVE: Establish jeg, du, han, hun, den, det, vi, I and de in natural use.

DA-03 — Present of være
OBJECTIVE: Establish er in identification and description while showing that finite verbs do not change by grammatical person.

DA-04 — Indefinite articles en and et
OBJECTIVE: Establish common versus neuter gender through useful noun phrases.

DA-05 — Predicative adjective agreement
OBJECTIVE: Establish common singular, neuter singular and plural adjective forms in natural predicates.

DA-06 — Attributive adjective agreement
OBJECTIVE: Establish adjective forms inside indefinite noun phrases.

DA-07 — Suffixed definite article: singular
OBJECTIVE: Establish common -en and neuter -et definite nouns through familiar vocabulary. The micro cards huset and bilen are previews.

DA-08 — Basic negation with ikke
OBJECTIVE: Establish ikke in simple main clauses with familiar predicates.

DA-09 — Yes/no questions and inversion
OBJECTIVE: Establish finite-verb-first polar questions and contrast them with declarative V2 clauses.

DA-10 — Plural nouns
OBJECTIVE: Establish representative productive plural patterns including -er, -e and zero plurals through lexical examples.

DA-11 — Plural adjective agreement
OBJECTIVE: Combine plural noun phrases and predicates with adjective -e.

DA-12 — Suffixed definite article: plural
OBJECTIVE: Establish common plural definite forms in natural noun phrases and sentences.

DA-13 — Demonstratives and definiteness
OBJECTIVE: Establish den, det, de and common demonstrative noun phrases while preparing for preposed definiteness with modifiers.

DA-14 — Early noun-phrase and clause synthesis
OBJECTIVE: Combine gender, articles, adjective agreement, definiteness, plurals, pronouns, negation and questions with expanding vocabulary.

STAGE 2 — FIRST VERBAL SYSTEM AND V2

DA-15 — Infinitives with at
OBJECTIVE: Establish lexical verb forms with at as useful dictionary/citation anchors.

DA-16 — Present tense with -r and common irregulars
OBJECTIVE: Establish ordinary present formation across representative verbs while emphasizing the absence of person/number agreement.

DA-17 — High-frequency verbs
OBJECTIVE: Expand være, have, gøre, gå, komme, se, vide, ville, kunne and related verbs through natural clauses.

DA-18 — Verbal negation and questions
OBJECTIVE: Extend ikke and inversion across familiar present verbs.

DA-19 — Question words
OBJECTIVE: Establish hvem, hvad, hvor, hvornår, hvordan, hvorfor and hvor meget/mange progressively in natural questions.

DA-20 — Main-clause V2 after fronted adverbials
OBJECTIVE: Establish inversion after sentence-initial time, place and other adverbials.

DA-21 — Other first-position constituents and V2
OBJECTIVE: Broaden the first-position slot beyond subjects and adverbials without treating word order as arbitrary.

DA-22 — Present/V2 synthesis
OBJECTIVE: Combine lexical expansion, finite verbs, questions, negation and V2 in varied everyday clauses.

STAGE 3 — DEFINITE MODIFIED NOUN PHRASES, POSSESSION AND OBJECTS

DA-23 — Preposed definite article + adjective
OBJECTIVE: Establish den/det/de + adjective + noun as in det store hus and den røde bil and contrast it with suffixed definiteness.

DA-24 — Possessive determiners
OBJECTIVE: Establish min/mit/mine, din/dit/dine, hans, hendes, vores, jeres and deres through natural noun phrases.

DA-25 — Possession with genitive -s
OBJECTIVE: Establish productive possessor constructions with names and nouns.

DA-26 — Direct and indirect object pronouns
OBJECTIVE: Introduce mig, dig, ham, hende, den, det, os, jer and dem in ordinary clauses.

DA-27 — Object position and pronoun placement
OBJECTIVE: Develop ordinary object placement relative to finite verbs, adverbs and other constituents.

DA-28 — Core prepositions
OBJECTIVE: Establish i, på, til, fra, med, for, af and other high-value prepositions in concrete contexts.

DA-29 — Location, direction and source
OBJECTIVE: Develop place relations through common verbs and prepositions.

DA-30 — Stage synthesis
OBJECTIVE: Combine modified noun phrases, possession, objects, prepositions and V2 with expanding vocabulary.

Once the syllabus has been substantially traversed, use authentic contemporary Danish writing and speech for backward validation. Authentic sources diagnose productive gaps; they do not automatically become flashcards.

GLOBAL COMPLETION AND REUSE RULES

1. Unit completion establishes canonical coverage rather than learner mastery.
2. Established vocabulary and grammar become reusable; later cards increasingly combine them in natural sentences.
3. New structural concepts normally appear first with controlled lexical load while vocabulary continues growing.
4. Stable live card IDs are not renumbered merely because syllabus organization changes.
5. Previewing a later construction does not move the Manifest frontier.
6. Parallel units may interleave once prerequisites are satisfied.

CURRENT SEED ALIGNMENT

The 15-card micro deck previews several later systems. The expanded 100-card seed establishes DA-01 through DA-16: core vocabulary, personal pronouns, være, en/et gender, predicative and attributive adjective agreement, singular suffixed definiteness, negation, yes/no questions and inversion, representative plurals, plural adjective agreement, plural definiteness, demonstratives, infinitive citation forms and the productive present-tense pattern.
