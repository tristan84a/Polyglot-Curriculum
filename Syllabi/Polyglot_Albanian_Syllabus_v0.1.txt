PURPOSE
A prerequisite-aware introduction sequence for modern Albanian. The first 15 standardized micro cards remain unchanged. The 100-card seed establishes the core of ordinary clauses and noun phrases. Vocabulary and grammar grow in a widening spiral, with declining paradigm density.

REFERENCE AND AUTHORING
Use contemporary standard spelling with ë and ç. Ordinary spoken standard Albanian is the initial production baseline. Gheg, Kosovo Albanian and regional variation enter later as recognizable varieties rather than being silently mixed into targets.
Write idiomatic English prompts and natural Albanian answers. Defaults are silent; parenthetical scaffolding is rare, especially beyond the opening. English he/she does not need extra gender labels when context supplies the distinction. Albanian normally allows subject omission; introduce independent pronouns but increasingly prefer natural omitted subjects. Preserve stable IDs. Avoid commas in example sentences where practical. Audit all EnglishPrompt values and IDs across the full CSV.

Albanian nominal morphology must be taught through actual nouns: suffixal definiteness, number and case interact. Adjectives normally follow nouns and many require linking articles i/e/të/së. Distinguish these articles from the definite noun suffix. Verbs are ordinarily cited in their present first-person singular form (punoj, lexoj, flas), not an invented English-style infinitive. Use one representative present paradigm then progressively more contextual forms. Treat active/nonactive, clitic placement, past tenses, subjunctive, admirative and Gheg infinitival variation longitudinally.

DEVELOPMENT POLICY
A unit represents introduction, not exhaustive mastery. Recycle earlier patterns with new vocabulary and combine them with newly introduced grammar. Initial paradigms may be dense if they teach genuine contrasts; subsequent verb families should be sampled selectively. Noun-case forms should appear through natural useful phrases before abstract full tables. Lexical expansion is continuous across all stages.
