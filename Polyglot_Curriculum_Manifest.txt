1. PURPOSE

This manifest is the compact progress and allocation record for the Polyglot curriculum repository.

The Polyglot Card Generation Protocol is the primary operating document for card generators. Each established language syllabus provides that language's sequence of introductions; the Charter provides top-level project purpose; and each language CSV is authoritative for cards that actually exist and for their row order.

The manifest records stable language ID allocations, current card-file extent, syllabus/frontier information where applicable, and concise checkpoints. Micro decks have no active frontier until they deliberately graduate into systematic syllabus-governed development.

Polyglot_Card_Generation_Protocol_v1.0.txt
