POLYGLOT CURRICULUM MANIFEST

Status: Current
Encoding: UTF-8

1. PURPOSE

This manifest is the compact progress and allocation record for the
Polyglot curriculum repository.

It does not define pedagogy. The Polyglot Card Generation Protocol is the
primary operating document for card generators. Each language syllabus
provides that language's sequence of introductions; the Charter provides
top-level project purpose and clarification; and the language CSV is
authoritative for cards that actually exist and for their row order.

The manifest records stable language ID allocations, current card-file
extent, syllabus version, the next syllabus frontier for forward
construction, and a concise checkpoint. Routine card generation may update
this manifest without amending the Charter or a language syllabus.

Current generation protocol:
Polyglot_Card_Generation_Protocol_v0.9.txt

A fresh generator extending an established language should normally begin
with the Protocol, then read this language's Manifest entry, syllabus and
current CSV. Other language decks are optional examples rather than
authorities.
