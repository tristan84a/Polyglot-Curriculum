POLYGLOT CURRICULUM MANIFEST

Status: Current
Encoding: UTF-8

1. PURPOSE

This manifest is the compact progress and allocation record for the Polyglot curriculum repository.

The Polyglot Card Generation Protocol is the primary operating document for card generators. Each established language syllabus provides that language's sequence of introductions; the Charter provides top-level project purpose; and each language CSV is authoritative for cards that actually exist and for their row order.

The manifest records one authoritative registry entry per language: stable ID allocation, CSV path, syllabus/frontier information where applicable, reference conventions, and concise checkpoints. Actual card counts, used IDs, and row order are authoritative only in the CSV files. Micro decks have no active frontier until they deliberately graduate into systematic syllabus-governed development.

Current generation protocol:
Polyglot_Card_Generation_Protocol_v1.0.txt
